Who We Are

Philippine-based, built for the road.

TOC Trucking Services moves cargo, furniture, and full households across the Philippines — by land and by inter-island ferry. From a single delivery to a complete house move, our team plans each trip around the way your cargo actually needs to be handled.

Every job is quoted around real factors: distance, truck size, cargo type, and how easy the pickup and delivery points are to reach — so what you're quoted is what reflects the actual job.
